# Business Recommendations — Saudi Real Estate Market (2024)
Based on: MOJ Sales Transactions 2024 (243,871 records) — descriptive analysis only

---

### 1. Segment investment strategy by "growth" vs. "stability" regions, not by price level alone
The data shows price level and price momentum are independent — Hail, Al Jouf, and
Tabuk delivered 30–54% price growth in 2024 despite being among the cheapest regions,
while Qassim and Makkah (higher-priced) actually declined. Investors optimizing for
**capital appreciation** should shortlist Hail/Al Jouf/Tabuk; investors optimizing for
**capital preservation and easy resale** should stay in Riyadh/Eastern Province, where
liquidity is consistently highest across all four quarters.

### 2. Treat Riyadh liquidity dominance as both an opportunity and a concentration risk
Riyadh represents 28% of national transaction volume and 41% of national transaction
value from a single region. For portfolio-style exposure, over-concentration in Riyadh
mirrors a single-asset risk profile — diversifying a portion of holdings into the
second liquidity tier (Makkah, Eastern Province) preserves resale flexibility while
reducing dependence on one regional market cycle.

### 3. Time acquisitions around the Ramadan demand dip
Transaction volume falls ~32% in the month of Ramadan across virtually every region,
then rebounds immediately. Buyers with flexible timing can use this seasonal trough as
a lower-competition entry window; sellers, conversely, should avoid listing during this
period and instead target the Q3–Q4 volume peak (July–December) when buyer activity is
highest.

### 4. Use area-to-price correlation strength as a due-diligence signal, not a valuation shortcut
In regions where area explains price strongly (Tabuk 0.92, Makkah 0.84), simple
price-per-m² comparisons are a reasonably reliable way to spot over/under-priced
listings. In regions where the correlation is weak (Najran 0.08, Madinah 0.19),
price-per-m² comparisons are unreliable — location, zoning, and property-specific
factors dominate, and any acquisition there needs manual, location-specific
due diligence rather than a formulaic m² comparison.

### 5. Match product type to regional demand mix before entering a new market
Tabuk's commercial share (19.9%) is more than double the national average — signaling
real commercial-sector demand rather than a purely residential story. Developers or
investors targeting commercial assets should prioritize Tabuk and Eastern Province
(12.3% commercial) over regions like Makkah (7.6%) or Jazan (5.4%), where the market
is overwhelmingly residential and commercial product would face a much thinner
buyer pool.

---

**Scope note:** These recommendations are derived entirely from observed 2024
transaction patterns (price, volume, momentum, mix) — no predictive model or future
price forecast was used. They describe *what happened* and where the data suggests
opportunity, not a guaranteed future outcome.
